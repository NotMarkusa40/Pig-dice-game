Pig Game (Python): A simple, interactive command-line implementation of the classic dice game Pig, built in Python.   Game Rules: Players: 2 to 4 players.   Objective: Be the first player to reach or exceed a total score of 30 points.   Gameplay: On your turn, you can choose to roll the dice as many times as you like, accumulating points for your current turn.   If you roll a 1, you lose all points accumulated during that turn, and your turn ends immediately.   If you choose to hold (enter anything other than 'y'), your current turn score is added to your total score, and the turn passes to the next player.   Features: Dynamic player setup supporting 2 to 4 players.   Custom player_scores sequence class to safely track and validate player scores.   Real-time turn tracking and score reporting.   How to Run: Make sure you have Python 3 installed on your system. Download or clone the project file (random_project.py).   Open your terminal or command prompt and run the script
